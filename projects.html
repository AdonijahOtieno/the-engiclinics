Hybrid CNC machine

A transportable CNC machine that combines wood routing and plasma cutting, built from sheet metal with bolted joints so it could be shipped and assembled on site.

Client
Kenya Red Cross Society, Lamu
Tools & methods
SolidWorks · Sheet metal · Welding · Bolted assembly
Outcome
Delivered and assembled on site in Lamu, giving the client one machine for both timber and metal cutting.

Engineering scope

  • Sheet-metal and mechanical component design
  • Design for assembly and transportation
  • Structural integrity assessment and FEA
  • Fabrication and on-site assembly

Checking fixtures & gauges

A checking fixture for a formed tube and a check gauge for a moulded part, so every production part can be verified against the design in seconds.

Tools & methods
SolidWorks · Machined steel and aluminium · Standard toggle clamps
Outcome
Operators can load a part, clamp it and see at a glance whether it is within tolerance, without measuring tools.

Engineering scope

  • Tube checking fixture on a machined base plate with levelling feet
  • End supports with locating pins and a toggle clamp to hold the tube in its checked position
  • Colour-coded datum and check blocks along the bend profile
  • Part check gauge: nest block with locating pins on a footed base plate
  • Full 3D CAD assemblies ready for machining and inspection

Automotive & e-mobility · Jigs & fixtures

Centre stand, side stand & step fixtures

Dedicated fixtures for three motorcycle parts: the centre stand, the side stand and the rear right-hand footstep. Each one locates the part on the same datums every time, so production stays consistent.

Tools & methods
3D CAD · Machined steel locators · Bolted modular blocks
Outcome
Repeatable part location for three high-volume motorcycle components, from one consistent fixture design approach.

Engineering scope

  • Centre stand fixture: locating pins, V-blocks and support towers for the A-frame and its pivot tube
  • Side stand fixture: end stops, a centre locator and a pivot pin holding the stand leg in position
  • Rear RH step fixture: tall support columns and pivot pins locating the bracket and footstep loop
  • Modular steel blocks bolted to flat base plates for easy adjustment and repair
  • Full 3D assemblies with each part modelled in place

Automotive & e-mobility · Quality

Pillion footrests & checking gauge

Left and right pillion footrest brackets for a motorcycle, with a dedicated checking gauge that verifies both parts against the design before they are fitted.

Tools & methods
SolidWorks weldments · Tube bending · Machined fixture blocks
Outcome
Each footrest pair can be checked for mounting-boss position and tube profile in a single loading, with no measuring tools.

Engineering scope

  • Mirror-image left and right footrest weldments in bent tube with cross-bars
  • Pivot bosses at each end for mounting to the frame
  • Checking gauge that holds both parts on locating pins through their bosses
  • Support blocks and stops along each tube to confirm the bend profile
  • Base plate with mounting holes for bench use

Electric motorcycle R&D
