Who we are

Precision-engineered. Production-proven.

EngiClinics is a mechanical design and manufacturing-engineering consultancy. For seven years we have turned concepts into production-ready hardware: parametric 3D CAD, GD&T-driven production drawings, FEA-validated structures, and the jigs, fixtures and gauges that hold tolerance at volume. More than 1,000 products and jobs delivered, across 100+ industries, from Nairobi and Kigali.

  • DFM and DFA from day one
  • GD&T to ISO standards
  • FEA-validated structures
  • Fixtures for repeatable quality
