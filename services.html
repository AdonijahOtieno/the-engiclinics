Our services

A complete mechanical design chain: take one step or let us carry your product all the way to production.

Concept design

We explore the idea with sketches, layouts and quick 3D concepts, then test them against cost, function and how they will be made, before any money goes into tooling.

  • You receive
  • Concept sketches and layouts
  • Early 3D concept models
  • Feasibility and risk notes

3D CAD modelling

Clean, parametric part and assembly models with proper design intent, so later changes are quick and safe rather than a rebuild.

  • You receive
  • Native CAD files plus STEP
  • Assemblies with bill of materials
  • Photoreal renders on request

Production drawings

Fully dimensioned and toleranced drawings in millimetres, with clear datums and GD&T, ready to hand to a machine shop, fabricator or overseas supplier.

  • You receive
  • Part and assembly drawings (PDF + DWG)
  • Tolerances and surface finishes
  • Revision-controlled drawing sets

Jig & fixture design

Workholding, welding and assembly aids that make your production repeatable, faster and safer for the people running it.

  • You receive
  • Fixture models and drawings
  • Locating and clamping scheme
  • Build-ready part list

Check gauge design

Custom gauges that close the loop between the digital model and the physical part, so your team can verify critical features on the shop floor in seconds.

  • You receive
  • Go / no-go and attribute gauges
  • Gauge tolerancing to standard
  • Inspection instructions

Design for manufacture

A fresh engineering review of an existing design to cut cost, reduce part count and remove the problems that only show up in production.

  • You receive
  • DFM review report
  • Recommended design changes
  • Updated models and drawings

FEA & engineering analysis

Finite element analysis, load-path studies and dimensional reviews that show how a part or structure behaves under real loads, so we fix weak points on screen rather than in the field.

  • You receive
  • Static stress and deflection studies
  • Load-path and mounting-point review
  • Clear report with recommended changes

Prototyping & validation

We support fabrication, assembly and fitment of the first builds, then use testing and operational data to validate the design and drive the next iteration.

  • You receive
  • Fabrication and assembly support
  • Fitment and field testing
  • Test findings fed back into the design
