Our story

Qualified engineers, built around your product

Founded in 2019, EngiClinics is a team of qualified engineers headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya, with an office in Kigali, Rwanda. Our vision is to be the leading provider of innovative, high-quality mechanical product design services, and we offer everything from concept design to production drawings, so the product development process is streamlined for our clients.

Armed with knowledge of current market dynamics and industry standards, we execute CAD projects with professionalism, and we keep researching the many roles CAD plays in engineering.

Our approach is evidence-backed: simulation guides the design, while physical testing and operational data validate the assumptions, expose failure modes and drive each iteration.

Mission

Our team bridges the gap between imagination and function by turning client visions into high-quality, manufacturable mechanical products, streamlining development from concept design to production drawings.

Vision

To be recognised as the leading provider of innovative, high-quality mechanical product design services, shaping the future of the industry through continuous learning and exceptional client partnerships.

Values

Precision in every drawing. Verifiable quality on every part. Continuous learning. Partnerships that last beyond one project.

Meet the founder

Adonijah Otieno Ogara

Founder, EngiClinics Consulting

Adonijah founded EngiClinics in 2019 to give manufacturers in East Africa and beyond access to world-class mechanical design: engineering that is right first time and built to be made.

He leads every project from concept through CAD, analysis, fixturing and validation, and holds the team to one standard: designs that are robust, manufacturable and fit for purpose.
